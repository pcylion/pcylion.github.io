About Easy Bill Maker

Easy Bill Maker is a free billing and invoicing tool built for small shopkeepers, retailers, and freelancers who need to create clean, professional bills quickly — without expensive software or a learning curve. Enter item names, quantities, and unit prices (per kg, per litre, or per piece), and the app calculates totals, applies discounts or tax, and produces a print-ready or PDF bill in seconds.

Who is this for?

Small retail shops, grocery and provision stores, home-based businesses, and freelancers who currently write bills by hand or use spreadsheets and want something faster, without switching to complex accounting software.

Key features

  • Instant bill calculation with support for per-kg, per-litre, and per-piece pricing
  • Print any bill or save it as a PDF
  • Share bills as a photo or PDF directly through WhatsApp
  • Automatic, editable bill numbering
  • "My Bills" keeps a copy of every bill you create, free of charge
  • An optional Pro plan adds automatic sales tracking, exportable reports (Excel, PDF, Word), and item/customer popularity insights

Frequently asked questions

Is Easy Bill Maker free to use?

Yes. Creating, printing, and sharing bills, along with the "My Bills" record-keeping feature, is completely free. A separate Pro subscription unlocks sales analytics and reporting for shop owners who want deeper insight into their business.

Where is my data stored?

All bill and shop information is stored locally in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and your data stays private to that device.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. Easy Bill Maker works in any modern mobile or desktop browser and can be added to your phone's home screen like an app.

Do I need an internet connection?

You'll need an internet connection to load the app initially. Once loaded, creating and calculating bills doesn't depend on a constant connection.
