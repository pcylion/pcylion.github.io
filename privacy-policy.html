Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Easy Bill Maker ("the app", "we") is a billing and invoicing tool for shopkeepers, retailers, and freelancers. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. Using the app means you agree to the practices described here.

How you sign in

Easy Bill Maker uses Google Sign-In (via Google Firebase Authentication) as the only way to create an account. We never see or store your Google password — Google handles the sign-in itself. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and (if you have one set) your Google account's profile photo, which Google provides to identify your account.

What we store, and where

Once you're signed in, the following information is saved to your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a secure cloud database operated by Google, so it's backed up and available on any device you sign in on:

DataWhy it's stored
Shop or business name and details (address, phone, GST, notes)Appears on the bills you create
Bills you create ("My Bills" archive)So you can reopen and reprint past bills
Sales records, due/credit ("khata") entriesPowers the Pro Sales Dashboard and due-tracking features
Bill number counterSo bill numbers continue in sequence
Profile photo (only if you choose to upload one)Shown in your Profile tab; entirely optional
Pro subscription / free-trial statusDetermines which features are unlocked for your account

This data is private to your account. Our Firestore security rules only allow your own signed-in account to read or write your data — no other user of the app can see it.

Sharing bills via WhatsApp or SMS

When you share a bill, Easy Bill Maker opens WhatsApp or your phone's messaging app directly (using standard wa.me and sms: links) with the bill's image, PDF, or text ready to send. The app does not send anything itself and does not have access to your WhatsApp messages or contacts — you choose the recipient and tap send yourself, inside WhatsApp/Messages.

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app displays ads served by Google AdSense. AdSense may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads, including personalized ads based on your interests, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll be shown a consent choice (via Google's consent management platform) before any personalized ads are shown. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

You can review or change your ad personalization settings anytime at adssettings.google.com, and learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and cloud data storage
  • Google AdSense — ad delivery for free-tier users

We do not share your data with any other third party.

Your choices

  • Reset app (in the Profile tab) clears your bills, sales, dues, and shop details, while keeping your Pro/trial status intact.
  • Delete your account entirely — email us (below) and we'll permanently delete your account data from Firestore.
  • Log out anytime from the Profile tab; this doesn't delete any data, it just signs you out of that device.

Data security

Data in transit and at rest in Firebase is encrypted by Google's infrastructure. Access to your account data is restricted to your own signed-in session by our Firestore security rules. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

Easy Bill Maker is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.
