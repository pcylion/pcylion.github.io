Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Bill Maker ("the app", "we") handles information when you use it. Easy Bill Maker is provided free of charge, and this policy applies to visitors of pcylion.github.io/easy-bill-maker.

Information stored on your device

Easy Bill Maker does not have a server or database of its own. All information you enter — shop name and address, customer details, bill items, saved bills ("My Bills"), and any Pro sales records — is stored locally in your own browser's storage on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself, and we have no access to it.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Sharing features

If you use the WhatsApp or SMS sharing buttons, the bill content and, where provided, the customer's phone number are passed directly to WhatsApp or your device's messaging app to open a chat or pre-filled message. This happens directly between your device and those apps — the information does not pass through or get stored by Easy Bill Maker.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
